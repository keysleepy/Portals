Our Story

Baked fresh, shared with the neighborhood.

Wynne’s Pizza House Katorsé started as a simple promise: pizza that’s baked fresh every day, topped generously, and priced for everyone in Pintong Bukawe to enjoy. Every pie leaves the kitchen made to order, never sitting around waiting.

Pull up a seat at our outdoor tables, catch up with friends, and pair your slice with something from our Katorsé coffee & milktea counter next door.
